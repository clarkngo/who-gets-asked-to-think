Who Gets Asked to Think?

ThinkCheck · a pilot study · in progress (October 2026)

When novice programmers ask AI coding assistants for help, do the answers help them judge the code (by explaining how it works, stating its limits, or suggesting how to test it), or do they just hand over the code? And does that change with what the novice asks for, or the language they write in?

  1. How often do responses support evaluative judgment rather than just provide code?
  2. Does this differ between "just make it" and "help me learn" requests?
  3. Does it differ by language: English or Taglish (the everyday Tagalog–English mix)?
  4. Is the generated code correct when run against hidden tests?

This is an audit of AI models, not a study of learners. Methods, data, and results will be posted here as the pilot is completed. Source code: GitHub repository.
